id: railway-token
title: Get your Railway token
time: 2 minutes
cost: From US$5 a month
link: https://railway.com/account/tokens
---
1. Sign up at [railway.com](https://railway.com).
2. Pick the **Hobby** plan under [Plans](https://railway.com/workspace/plans) and add a card.
3. Open [Tokens](https://railway.com/account/tokens), name it `Setup`, leave **No workspace**, click **Create**.
4. Copy the token here.

> **Stuck?** Rejected? Make it again with **No workspace**.
