Railway is where your agency lives. You'll make an account, pick a plan, then copy one token.

1. Go to [railway.com](https://railway.com) and sign up. **Sign in with GitHub** is easiest. You'll land on a free trial, which is fine to start.
2. Open **Plans** ([railway.com/workspace/plans](https://railway.com/workspace/plans)), choose **Hobby** and add your card. The wizard needs Hobby to build your agency.
3. Open **Account Settings**, then **Tokens** ([railway.com/account/tokens](https://railway.com/account/tokens)).
4. Type a name like `Setup`. Leave **Workspace** on **No workspace**. Click **Create**.
5. Copy the token and paste it into the wizard. You'll only see it once.

You'll see a long line of letters and numbers. That's your token.

> **Stuck?** "Railway didn't accept that token": you made it with a workspace picked. Make a new one with **No workspace**. "Needs the Hobby plan": finish step 2, then press **Check** again.
