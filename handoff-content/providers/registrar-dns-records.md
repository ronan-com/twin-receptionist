title: Add the records yourself
time: 10 minutes, then up to a few hours
cost: Free
link: https://www.godaddy.com/help/add-an-a-record-19238
---
1. Open your domain's **DNS** page where you bought it.
2. Click **Add** and pick the **Type** shown here.
3. Copy the **Name** and **Value** across.
4. Repeat for each record. They tick green here.

> **Stuck?** Stays red? In **Name** type only the short part, like `app`.
