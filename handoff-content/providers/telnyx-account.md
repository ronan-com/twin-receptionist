## What it's for
Telnyx is the phone company your receptionists run on. The account is yours: you see every call and charge there, and you pay Telnyx directly.

## Steps
1. Open Telnyx sign-up and register with your work email.
2. Click the link in the email Telnyx sends you.
3. In Telnyx, click your profile icon (top right), open **Account Level** and click **Upgrade**.
4. Confirm who you are with **LinkedIn** or **GitHub**.
5. Verify your mobile number and turn on two-step sign-in (2FA).
6. Enter your business address.
7. Click your profile icon and open **Billing**.
8. Click **Make Payment** and pay by card. The minimum is US$10.
9. Once that payment has gone through, turn on **Auto Recharge** on the same page and set a **Threshold** and a **Recharge** amount.
10. Check that your **Account Level** now says **Paid**.

## What to paste into the wizard
Nothing from this step. The next step creates the API key you paste.

## Permissions / scopes
None.

## Watch out for
- Telnyx says a business email is required to sign up. Free addresses such as Gmail may be refused.
- A new account starts as a trial. Real callers can't get through until you upgrade and pay, so don't skip steps 3 to 10.
- You get two tries at the identity check, one with LinkedIn and one with GitHub. Each LinkedIn or GitHub login can verify only one Telnyx account.
- You can't add credit until the upgrade is done.
- Telnyx reviews a new card for up to 48 hours (not counting weekends). Watch for an email titled "Attention: Payment Method Allowed".
- New accounts can pay at most US$100 on the first day. The limit rises by US$50 a day.
- Card and PayPal payments carry a 3% fee.
- **Auto Recharge** only appears after your first manual payment. It tops you back up to Threshold plus Recharge, so one charge can be bigger than the Recharge amount.
- If credit runs out, your receptionists stop answering until you top up.
- Australian local numbers need an Australian address and proof of address dated within the last 3 months. Mobiles also need ID. Telnyx says it takes about 72 hours to approve them after you send the documents.
- Unconfirmed: Telnyx's help page calls the billing page **Billing Overview** in the top-right menu. If you don't see **Billing**, look for that.
