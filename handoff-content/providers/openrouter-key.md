1. Sign up at [openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/) with Google, GitHub or email.
2. Open [Credits](https://openrouter.ai/settings/credits) and add credit with a card. About US$10 is plenty to start; with at least US$10 you also get a higher daily limit on free models. Auto top-up is optional.
3. Open [Keys](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys) and click **Create Key**.
4. Name it, such as `Agency`. Leave the credit limit empty, or set one that is more than you will spend (when a key hits its limit it stops working).
5. Click **Create** and copy the key straight away. OpenRouter shows it once. It starts with `sk-or-v1-`.
6. Paste it into the setup page. Or use **Sign in with OpenRouter** instead, with no pasting.

## Common problems
- **Error 401 "invalid key".** The key was removed or disabled, or the copy has a hidden space or missing letters. Copy it again, or create a new one.
- **Error 402 "insufficient credits".** The balance is empty. Add credit on the Credits page, then retry.
- **Error 403, or "key limit reached".** The key's own credit limit is used up. Raise it on the Keys page, or make a new key.
- **Error 429.** Too many requests at once. Wait a minute and try again.
- **Error 502 or 503.** The AI model is down or busy. Try again soon: it fixes itself.
- **Key shows as expired.** Keys made during OpenRouter's onboarding can expire. Create a normal key under **Keys**.
