The key lets your agency answer calls and buy numbers on your Telnyx account.

1. In Telnyx, click your profile icon (top right), then **Account Settings**.
2. Click **API Keys**, then **Create API Key**.
3. Type a name like `Receptionist` and leave **Expiration** on **No Expiration**.
4. Copy the key straight away and paste it into the wizard. It starts with KEY and you'll only see it once.

The wizard then shows your Telnyx credit.

> **Stuck?** "Telnyx didn't accept that key": copy the whole line again, or make a new key. Calls not coming through: your **Account Level** must say **Paid** and you need credit.
