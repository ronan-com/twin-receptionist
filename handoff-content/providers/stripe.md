title: Get paid with Stripe
time: 20 minutes
cost: No monthly fee
link: https://dashboard.stripe.com/register
---
1. Sign up at [stripe.com](https://dashboard.stripe.com/register).
2. Verify your business and add your bank account.
3. Make a **Payment Link** with a monthly price.
4. Send it to your client.

> **Stuck?** First payout takes 7 to 14 days.
