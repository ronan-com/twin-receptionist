Optional. Resend sends sign-in links, invites and call notices from your own address. Skip it and invite links show on screen for you to copy.

1. Sign up at [resend.com](https://resend.com/signup) and confirm your email.
2. Click **API Keys**, then **Create API Key**.
3. Type a name like `Receptionist`. Set **Permission** to **Full access**.
4. Copy the key and paste it into the wizard. It starts with re_ and you'll only see it once.

The wizard adds your sending domain in Resend for you. You don't have to.

> **Stuck?** "That Resend key can only send email": you picked **Sending access**. Make a new key with **Full access**. Email set-up needs your own domain too (see the domain guide), or skip it for now.
