---
id: digitalocean-token
title: Get a DigitalOcean token
time: 5 minutes
cost: From US$18 a month
link: https://cloud.digitalocean.com/account/api/tokens
---
1. Sign up at [digitalocean.com](https://cloud.digitalocean.com/) and add a card.
2. Open **API**, then **Generate New Token**.
3. Choose **Full Access** and click **Generate**.
4. Copy the token here.

> **Stuck?** Rejected? Make a new one with **Full Access**.
