One key lets your agency answer calls and buy numbers on your Telnyx account.

1. Open the [API Keys page](https://portal.telnyx.com/#/api-keys) in Telnyx (or click your profile icon, then **Account Settings**, then **API Keys**).
2. Click **Create API Key** (top right).
3. Click the copy icon next to the new key. It starts with `KEY` and is shown only once.
4. Paste it into the **Telnyx API key** box and press **Check and continue**.

> **Stuck?** "Telnyx didn't accept that key": copy the whole line again, or make a new key. Calls not coming through: **Account Level** must say **Paid** and you need credit.
