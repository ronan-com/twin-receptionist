Twilio gives your receptionists Australian numbers and sends their texts.

1. Sign up at [twilio.com/try-twilio](https://www.twilio.com/try-twilio) and confirm your email and mobile.
2. In the [Twilio Console](https://console.twilio.com/) click **Upgrade** (top of the page) and add a card. A trial account can't buy Australian numbers.
3. On the Console home page, find **Account Info**. Copy the **Account SID** (starts with `AC`) into the **Twilio Account SID** box.
4. Next to it, click **Show** on the **Auth Token**, copy it, and paste it into the **Twilio Auth Token** box.
5. Before buying an Australian number, make a regulatory bundle under [Regulatory Compliance, Bundles](https://console.twilio.com/us1/develop/phone-numbers/regulatory-compliance/bundles). What it needs is on [Twilio's Australia page](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/au/regulatory).

> **Stuck?** "Twilio didn't accept those details": copy the Account SID and Auth Token from the same **Account Info** box again. Twilio says it needs a bundle: wait for the bundle to be approved, then buy the number.
