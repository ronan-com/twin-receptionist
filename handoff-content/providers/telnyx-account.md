id: telnyx-account
title: Open a Telnyx account
time: 10 minutes, then Telnyx's checks (hours to 2 days)
cost: Free to open. Add US$10 credit.
link: https://telnyx.com/sign-up
---
1. Sign up at [telnyx.com](https://telnyx.com/sign-up) and confirm your email.
2. In the [portal](https://portal.telnyx.com/) open **Account Level**, then **Upgrade**.
3. Add at least US$10 under **Billing**.
4. Wait for the approval email.

> **Stuck?** Not approved yet? Press **Skip for now** and connect it later.
