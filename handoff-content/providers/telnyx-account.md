Telnyx carries your calls. The account is yours and you pay Telnyx directly.

1. Open [telnyx.com/sign-up](https://telnyx.com/sign-up), sign up with your work email, and click the link in the email Telnyx sends you.
2. In the [Telnyx portal](https://portal.telnyx.com/) click your profile icon (top right), then **Account Level**, then **Upgrade**. Confirm who you are and add your mobile and business address.
3. Open **Billing**, click **Make Payment** and pay at least US$10 by card.
4. Wait for Telnyx's email saying your account is approved. **Account Level** then says **Paid**.

Waiting for approval? Press **Skip for now** on the Phones step. You paste the key later in your dashboard, under **Connections**.

> **Stuck?** Can't add credit: finish the upgrade in step 2 first. An Australian number later needs proof of your address (a bill from the last 3 months) and takes 1 to 3 days.
