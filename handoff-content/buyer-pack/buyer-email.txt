WHO OWNS WHAT
Everything runs in accounts in your name: your Railway project and database, your Telnyx phone account, and any domain, Resend email or calendar apps you add. You pay each provider directly.

Once your install finishes, our setup site deletes every key you gave it. We keep no login to your Railway project or to any of your provider accounts.

Two things are ON by default so we can help you fast. Both are your choice: you can switch either one off at any time in your dashboard under Settings, Support, and it takes effect straight away.
- Health and usage stats: every 15 minutes your agency sends us its version, whether it is healthy, counts (clients, receptionists, phone numbers, calls and bookings over recent days, errors), a short list of what went wrong in the last 24 hours (a kind and a one-line description with numbers, emails and names masked), whether automatic updates are on, plus your brand name and logo address and a yes or no for your optional extras. It never sends caller names or numbers, messages, transcripts, recordings, customer details, passwords or keys. We use it to spot a problem before you do, and we're alerted if your agency goes quiet. Turn it off and nothing is sent.
- Support access: ON for a fresh install, so we can sign in to your dashboard and help. We sign in through a one-time link. While access is on, we can see what's in your dashboard (including calls and messages), and we can also make changes for you: receptionists, automations, phone numbers (which can include buying a number on your Telnyx account), knowledge, clients, branding and settings. If you'd rather we only look, switch "Support can make changes" off. Even with access on, we can never see or change your keys, your billing, your owner account or password, your team, delete your agency or export your data. Every sign-in and every change is written to your audit log, marked "By support", and turning access off ends any open session at once.

Switch both off and nothing leaves your agency, and we have no way in.

Automatic updates (Settings, Updates) are also ON: overnight between 2 and 4am your time, or as soon as a version is available if you prefer. Your agency updates itself inside your own Railway project, using a token that only works for that one project and that only your agency holds. A version that fails to build never replaces the one running, and your data is untouched. Turn it off and you update by pressing "Update your agency" on your setup link, as before. If Railway won't let your agency update itself, Settings says automatic updates are unavailable and "Update your agency" works the same way.

Billing centres is separate. Set up your own Stripe account and enter your identity and bank details directly with Stripe. Never send them to us.
