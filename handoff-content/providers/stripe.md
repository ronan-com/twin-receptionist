Optional, and not connected to the app. Stripe is how you charge your own clients. There's nothing to paste into the wizard.

1. Sign up at [stripe.com](https://dashboard.stripe.com/register) and confirm your email. Turn on two-step sign-in.
2. Verify your business and add your bank account under **Payout settings**.
3. For a monthly fee, click **Payment Links**, **+New**, **+Add a new product**. Set the price to recurring monthly, then **Create link**.
4. For a one-off bill, click **Invoices**, **Create Invoice**.
5. Send the link or invoice to your client.

> **Stuck?** Can't take real payments yet: Stripe has to finish verifying your business first. Your first payout takes 7 to 14 days.
