Telnyx is the phone company that carries your calls. The account is yours and you pay Telnyx directly.

1. Sign up at [telnyx.com](https://telnyx.com/sign-up) with your work email, then click the link in the email Telnyx sends.
2. Click your profile icon (top right), open **Account Level** and click **Upgrade**. Confirm who you are with **LinkedIn** or **GitHub**, then add your mobile and your business address.
3. Open **Billing** and click **Make Payment**. Pay at least US$10 by card.
4. Check **Account Level** now says **Paid**.

Telnyx can take up to 2 working days to approve a new card. You'll get an email when it's done.

> **Stuck?** Can't add credit: finish the upgrade in step 2 first. Getting an Australian number later? It needs proof of your address (a bill dated in the last 3 months) and takes 1 to 3 days to approve.
