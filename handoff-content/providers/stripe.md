1. Sign up at [stripe.com](https://dashboard.stripe.com/register) and confirm your email.
2. Click **Activate payments** and give your business details and ID. Add the bank account you want paying into.
3. Open **Payment Links > New** and add a product with a monthly recurring price.
4. Copy the link and send it to your client. Stripe emails you each time they pay.

## Common problems
- **Can't take payments yet.** Stripe still needs details. Open the **Activate payments** banner and finish every item.
- **No money in the bank.** The first payout takes about 7 to 14 days. After that it is faster.
- **A client's card is declined.** Stripe emails them to update it. You can also retry from the payment in the dashboard.
- **This is optional.** Your agency works without Stripe: it is only for billing your own clients.
