id: resend
title: Get a Resend key
time: 3 minutes
cost: Free for 100 emails a day
link: https://resend.com/api-keys
---
1. Sign up at [resend.com](https://resend.com/signup).
2. Open **API Keys**, then **Create API Key**.
3. Choose **Full access**.
4. Copy the key (starts with `re_`).

> **Stuck?** Rejected? Make a new key with **Full access**.
