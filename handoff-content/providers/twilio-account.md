---
id: twilio-account
title: Twilio details
time: 10 minutes
cost: Free to open. Numbers and texts come from credit.
link: https://console.twilio.com/
---
1. Sign up at [twilio.com](https://www.twilio.com/try-twilio) and click **Upgrade**.
2. In the [Console](https://console.twilio.com/) copy the **Account SID** (starts with `AC`).
3. Click **Show** on **Auth Token** and copy it.
4. For Australian numbers, make a [regulatory bundle](https://console.twilio.com/us1/develop/phone-numbers/regulatory-compliance/bundles) first.

> **Stuck?** Rejected? Copy both from the same **Account Info** box.
