---
id: registrar-nameservers
title: Change your nameservers
time: 5 minutes, then a few hours
cost: Free
link: https://developers.cloudflare.com/dns/zone-setups/full-setup/setup/
---
1. Sign in where you bought the domain.
2. Open its **Nameservers** setting.
3. Choose **Custom**.
4. Replace them with the two from Cloudflare and save.

> **Stuck?** Can't find it? Ask your registrar how to set custom nameservers.
