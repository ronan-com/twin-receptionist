---
id: cloudflare-domain
title: Put your domain on Cloudflare
time: 15 minutes, then a few hours
cost: Free
link: https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up
---
1. Sign up free at [cloudflare.com](https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up).
2. Click **Onboard a domain**, type it, pick the **Free** plan.
3. Copy the two nameservers Cloudflare shows.
4. Set them where you bought the domain.

> **Stuck?** Still pending after a day? Check both nameservers match.
