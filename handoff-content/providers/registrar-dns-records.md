Optional. Use this if you'd rather not move your domain to Cloudflare. The wizard shows each record and ticks it green when it appears.

1. Keep the wizard open on the list of records.
2. In another tab, sign in where you bought your domain and open its **DNS** or **DNS records** page.
3. Click **Add** (GoDaddy: **Add New Record**). Pick the **Type** the wizard shows.
4. Copy the **Name** and **Value** from the wizard into the boxes. For **Name**, type only the short part (like app, not the whole address). Leave **TTL** as it is. Save.
5. Repeat for each record, then wait for the ticks. Most appear within 15 minutes.

**GoDaddy:** **Domain Portfolio**, your domain, **DNS** tab. **Namecheap:** **Domain List**, **Manage**, **Advanced DNS**. **Crazy Domains:** **Account Manager**, **DNS Settings**. **VentraIP:** **VIPcontrol**, **Domains**, **DNS**.

> **Stuck?** A record stays red: the most common mistake is typing the full address in **Name**. Type just app. If there's already an old record with the same name and type, edit it instead of adding a second.
