---
id: openrouter-key
title: Get an OpenRouter key
time: 2 minutes
cost: Free to open. Add a few dollars of credit.
link: https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys
---
1. Sign up at [openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai/) and add credit under **Credits**.
2. Open [Keys](https://openrouter.ai/settings/keys) and click **Create Key**.
3. Copy the key (starts with `sk-or-`).
4. Paste it here.

> **Stuck?** Rejected? Make a new key and copy it once.
