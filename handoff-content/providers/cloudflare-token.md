---
id: cloudflare-token
title: Get a Cloudflare token
time: 3 minutes
cost: Free
link: https://dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens
---
1. Open [API Tokens](https://dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens) and click **Create Token**.
2. Use the **Edit zone DNS** template and add **Zone**, **Zone**, **Read**.
3. Choose your domain under **Zone Resources**, then **Create Token**.
4. Copy the token here.

> **Stuck?** Never paste your Global API Key.
