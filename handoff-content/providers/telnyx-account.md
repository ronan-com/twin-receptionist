1. Sign up at [telnyx.com](https://telnyx.com/sign-up) with a work email (not Gmail or Hotmail) and confirm it.
2. In the [portal](https://portal.telnyx.com/) click your profile icon, then **Account Level**, then **Upgrade**. New accounts start with a free trial that cannot take real calls.
3. Pass the identity check with LinkedIn or GitHub (you get one try with each). Then verify your mobile number, turn on two-factor sign-in and add your service address.
4. Add a card or PayPal, then add credit under **Billing**. The minimum payment is US$10. Calls and numbers draw from this balance, so keep it topped up.
5. Check **Account Level**: it should no longer say Trial. Some accounts show a **Verifications** page instead: you need Level 1 (done when your email is confirmed). Level 2 lifts payment and messaging limits. It needs a company name and phone number in **Account Information** and can take 48 hours.
6. Buy an Australian number later from your agency dashboard (the **Connections** page). Local (landline) numbers need an Australian address and a bill or bank statement under 3 months old. Mobile numbers also need an ID check. Approval takes about 72 hours.

## Common problems
- **Identity check rejected.** Try the other option (GitHub if LinkedIn failed, or the reverse). If both fail, open a new account with a business email address.
- **"Pretrial" account, no voice.** The first stage only has AI tools and cannot add funds. Finish **Upgrade** under **Account Level** to unlock calls.
- **Calls only work to one number.** That is the trial limit: one verified number, small credit. Upgrade and add funds to take real calls.
- **Number stuck on "pending requirements".** Telnyx still needs a document. Open the number's order, upload a proof of address under 3 months old, and wait up to 72 hours.
- **Can't buy a number.** Accounts at the first verification level can only order local numbers in their own country. Finish verification to lift that.
- **Texts to Australia don't send.** Open the number's **Messaging Profile**. Australia must be in its allowed destination countries, and a sender ID set. Alphanumeric sender IDs sent to Australia must be registered with the ACMA register or they show as "Unverified".
- **Calls stop later.** Your balance reached zero. Add funds under **Billing** and turn on auto-recharge.
